Diabetic-retinopathy grading pipeline

Stage 1 quality - Stage 2 preprocessing - Stage 3 vessels - Stage 4 lesions - ConvNeXt-Tiny + CORN grader (3 seeds) - uncertainty - explanations

Research prototype - not a medical device; its output is not a diagnosis. Explanation maps show where the score is sensitive; they do not show that lesions were detected. Uploaded images are deleted after the run.
